# 75-Second Judge Demo Script

## 0–8s — Problem
"Business data can show what happened, but teams still have to manually turn dashboards and spreadsheets into a decision. Decisio is built to shorten that gap."

## 8–20s — Upload
Open the live app and show **Drop CSV / XLSX here**.
Say: "I can bring in a normal business CSV or Excel workbook. Decisio reads the table and works from the observed data."

## 20–35s — Ask
Click **Revenue by region**.
Say: "Instead of manually building another pivot table, I ask a business question. Decisio computes the answer and exposes the evidence behind it."

Pause on the answer and evidence table.

## 35–48s — Another question
Click **Margin by category**.
Say: "The same dataset can be explored from another decision angle. Margin is explicitly calculated as profit divided by revenue."

## 48–65s — Simulate
Click **10% price scenario**, then move the price slider.
Say: "Before acting, I can test a decision assumption. The simulator shows the scenario numbers and clearly labels the assumption rather than presenting it as a guaranteed forecast."

## 65–75s — Reliability
Point to **Decision reliability**.
Say: "The key idea is evidence first: traceable calculations, visible assumptions, and a guard against unsupported questions. Decisio turns data into a decision workflow."

## Final screen
Leave the simulator and evidence visible.
Do not spend time reading the README or code.

### Recording checklist
- Use the deployed URL.
- Start with the demo dataset already visible.
- Keep browser zoom at 100%.
- Do not scroll unnecessarily.
- Upload one small CSV/XLSX only if the upload step is important to show.
- Use one continuous recording.
- Keep the cursor visible when clicking.
- Target 60–90 seconds.
